"Shinobi Striker" Dev Letter #15 Released! "Kabuto Yakushi (Sage Mode)" Joins the Battle!!

#ShinobiStriker

What is "NARUTO TO BORUTO: SHINOBI STRIKER"!

The "Ninja World League" to determine the strongest ninja begins!
Acrobatic ninja battle action aiming for the strongest ninja in online multi-battles!!

Greetings

Hello, everyone in the ninja community!
This is the "NARUTO TO BORUTO: SHINOBI STRIKER" development team.
Thank you as always for playing "NARUTO TO BORUTO: SHINOBI STRIKER".

In this 15th development letter, we bring you information about the newly joining "Kabuto Yakushi (Sage Mode)" and new items!

"Kabuto Yakushi (Sage Mode)" Joins the Battle!!

"Kabuto Yakushi (Sage Mode)" controls Sage Chakra as a Heal-type character who disrupts enemies with excellent Ninjutsu and versatile attacks while supporting allies in battle.
Here are the Ninjutsu techniques of Kabuto Yakushi (Sage Mode), who shed his snake skin and ascended to a dragon!

Ninjutsu 1: "Sage Art: White Rage Jutsu"
Unleashes natural energy as a dragon of light, stopping enemy movement with intense light, sound, and air vibrations! When released, the dragon explodes upon contact with an enemy or after flying for a set duration, dealing damage to surrounding enemies along with vision impairment, hearing impairment, and defense reduction. Though the disruption effect is brief, it disorients targets and restricts their movement! Because the explosion cannot be guarded against and has a large area of effect, launching it into a melee fight allows you to disrupt multiple enemies at once!

Ninjutsu 2: "Transmission Shadow"
A technique that incorporates the cells of others into one's own body to freely manipulate their jutsu.
By varying inputs using the Ninjutsu button and movement stick, as well as ground or aerial positioning, the Jutsu behavior changes as follows:
・ Neutral (Ground/Air): Launches Orochimaru's snake body
・ Ground + Up: Attacks opponents from beneath their feet with bone spikes
・ Ground + Down: Creates a wall in front to defend
・ Aerial + Up: Sound Genjutsu that pierces terrain and opponent's guard
・ Aerial + Down: Restraining technique to block long-range enemy attacks

By utilizing each technique depending on the situation, this technical Ninjutsu allows you to adapt to various battle conditions! However, individual sub-technique power is slightly tuned down compared to dedicated Ninjutsu, so analyze the battlefield carefully to select the right technique for the job!

Secret Ninjutsu: "Sage Art: Inorganic Reincarnation"
A technique that uses natural energy to freely command non-living matter to attack!
Upon activation, the terrain protrudes to strike the target from a distance, after which the protruded terrain automatically continues attacking the targeted enemy for a set duration. The initial activation deals high damage capable of knocking out an enemy in a single blow, so aim for openings! The automatic attack portion continues over an extended time while the caster can act freely, enabling even greater power when combined with your own follow-up attacks!

The Kabuto Yakushi (Sage Mode) introduced here can also be tried out in the free update! Purchasing the Special Ninjutsu Training Master DLC allows your custom Avatar Ninja to equip his Ninjutsu!
Additionally, as a reward for this Special Ninjutsu Training, you can obtain the weapon "Kabuto's Sage Snake"! We've faithfully recreated a fighting style utilizing white snakes, so please enjoy it!

New SS+ Items Introduced!

Here is an introduction to the new SS+ items appearing in the scroll appraisal!

Heal-Type Weapon: Divine Sword "Ame-no-Minakanushi" (SS+)
A weapon themed after Kaguya Otsutsuki, the progenitor of chakra! Recreating her ability to manipulate dimensions freely, operating direction inputs during heavy attacks or holding the button performs different attacks:
・ Heavy Attack: Thrust attack (holding button greatly increases reach)
・ Up + Heavy Attack: Short-range lava attack
・ Right + Heavy Attack: Long-range White Zetsu attack on ground / Acid attack in air
・ Left + Heavy Attack: Summons floating icicles around you that automatically attack
・ Down + Heavy Attack: Restraining attack via Black Zetsu to obstruct target movement

Combining each attack enables powerful continuous combos and setups! Master her techniques to overwhelm enemies just like in the original series!

Defense-Type Weapon: Crow Katana "Usugumori" (SS+)
A new enchanted blade themed after Tengu, crystals, and dual-wielding! Double-blade strikes combined with crystal clones overwhelm foes. Furthermore, each hit with normal and heavy attacks earns Chakra Stacks, gradually enhancing physical capabilities as stacks build up! Continuous fighting on the front lines lets you slice through the battlefield with fluid combos!

Attack-Type Weapon: Thermal Outfit "Youth Unleashed!!" (SS+)
A weapon themed after Might Guy, Konoha's Noble Blue Beast! Recreating powerful taijutsu embodying his intense passion and burning youth. Holding heavy attack opens the "Gate of Wonder" (7th Gate), boosting physical stats and altering heavy attacks! Up input triggers "Morning Peacock" and Down input triggers "Daytime Tiger"! While the Gate of Wonder is active, you can crush oncoming foes, but beware: physical capabilities drop significantly once the effect ends!

Patch 2.72 Balance Adjustment Explanation

Here we explain the battle balance adjustments implemented in the 2.72 update.

・ Detailed Values Included for Balance Adjustments
In our recent maintenance balance update, we tested providing detailed patch notes. Previously, parameters like long-range tracking were left vague, but based on player feedback, from Patch 2.72 onward we are providing concrete numbers to clarify our intent and adjustments.

・ Reworks for Older Ninjutsu / Secret Ninjutsu & Individual Tweaks
This update focuses on reworking older Ninjutsu and Secret Ninjutsu. For example, "C2 Dragon" now allows an additional input to detonate on command or upon expiration. "Super Expansion Jutsu" now releases a shockwave upon giantifying to improve hit reliability.

Furthermore, cooldowns for "Dynamic Entry" and "One-Finger Hell Stab" have been reduced, while debuff durations for techniques like "Sage Art: Amphibian Technique" were shortened. Detailed notes will be available in-game!

New Items Introduction

SS Costumes: "Dosu Kinuta Style" & "Kin Tsuchi Style"
Recreating outfits of Sound Village assassins Dosu Kinuta and Kin Tsuchi!

Ranged-Type Weapon: Senbon "Haku"
A weapon themed after Haku, the ninja serving Zabuza! Recreates Ice Style combos and rapid Senbon surprise strikes. Hitting heavy attack extensions multiple times freezes the target!

Ninja Tool: "Rainstorm Senbon"
An umbrella ninja tool launching all-directional Senbon barrages over an expanding area!

Battle Road Update Announcement

Battle Road transitions to a new season in the next update!
Earn the original Heal-type weapon "Scientific Ninja Tool: Leaf Shield" along with Secret Scrolls and special titles like "Snake Doctor" and "Intellectual"!

Conclusion

Thank you for reading to the end!
The update arrives on Thursday, Nov 12, with "Kabuto Yakushi (Sage Mode)" releasing on Friday, Nov 13. Stay tuned!

See you in the next Dev Letter!
